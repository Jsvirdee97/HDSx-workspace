VISUAL AUDIT — NHANES BMI BY INCOME

Plot question
How did the unweighted mean BMI vary across the supplied NHANES cycles for
adults aged 20–80 in each income group?

Issues and categories

1. Category: Correctness — analysis cohort does not match the subtitle.
   The summary does not filter on Age, even though the subtitle says adults
   age 20–80. Corrected by including ages 20 through 80, inclusive, and
   excluding records with missing age.

2. Category: Correctness — most survey cycles are omitted.
   The summary keeps only the first and last cycles, so the plot cannot support
   a claim about change across every cycle. Corrected by using every cycle in
   the supplied file and ordering them chronologically. The supplied data has
   no 2019–2020 cycle.

3. Category: Design — the y-axis removes plotted values.
   Limits of 24–28 exclude many group means above 28, causing ggplot to drop
   observations and making the chart incomplete. Corrected with an axis that
   includes all plotted means and starts at zero.

4. Category: Interpretation — the title makes an unsupported causal claim.
   Grouped descriptive means do not show that low income drives BMI or obesity.
   Corrected with a neutral title describing the measure and comparison.

5. Category: Interpretation — the subtitle overstates the evidence.
   “Climbed steadily across every NHANES cycle” is inconsistent with the
   endpoint-only data shown and the fluctuations in the full cycle summaries.
   Corrected by showing every available cycle and describing the cohort and
   supplied date range without asserting a steady trend.

6. Category: Interpretation — “obesity” is not the plotted outcome.
   The plot displays mean BMI, not obesity prevalence or the number of adults
   meeting an obesity threshold. Corrected by labeling the outcome as mean BMI.

Final caption
Unweighted sample means; excludes records missing BMI, income group, or age.
The supplied file has no 2019–2020 cycle. Descriptive means do not establish
causation or account for the NHANES survey design.
