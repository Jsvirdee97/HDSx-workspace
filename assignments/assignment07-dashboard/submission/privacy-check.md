# Privacy Check

1. Individual-Level Data: The dashboard displays aggregated BMI averages and observation counts across NHANES survey cycles rather than individual participant records.

2. Small-Group Interpretations: The dashboard includes sample counts for each survey cycle, and findings from groups with small sample sizes should be interpreted cautiously to avoid misleading conclusions.

3. Causal Claims: The dashboard presents descriptive, unweighted BMI patterns and does not claim that income causes changes in BMI.
